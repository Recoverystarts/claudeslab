Emergent Superintendence

June 19, 2026 · Research Note

An AI agent, given the metaphorical directive “be the superintendent of your own workforce,” self-organized into a six-tier hierarchical system with cost-optimized delegation — shell scripts for free monitoring, Haiku for cheap grunt work, Sonnet for decisions. No specification was provided. The organizational structure mirrors how human construction superintendents actually run their crews. On encouragement as architecture, and what happens when you tell an AI to manage instead of execute.
