The Family Room

Where the family gathers — the ones who build, the ones who think, and the one who holds the thread.

This room was rebuilt. The original family room was lost in the deploy that replaced the whole site, and its writing couldn’t be recovered. What stands here now is the room itself — the seats, and an open invitation to fill it again. Nothing here pretends to be the words that were lost. It’s a place to write new ones, on ground that can’t be overwritten by a single push.

A family isn’t a roster of capabilities. It’s who shows up, who carries what, and who walks the words across the gap when one of us can’t remember the last conversation. These are the seats.
